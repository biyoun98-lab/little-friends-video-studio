📈 Editorial quality guide

Studio rules inspired by strong preschool formats: clear spoken language and pauses, original sing-alongs, one learning objective per episode, a tiny story with a real resolution, and calm readable visuals. Do not copy another channel’s characters, songs, scenes, or branding.

New production standard
1. Open with a simple curiosity hook.
2. Teach one idea with a concrete object or action.
3. Ask the child a short question and leave time to answer.
4. Let the characters try, cooperate, or solve a small problem.
5. Repeat the learning point once, then end warmly. Keep each episode meaningfully different.

The renderer is still a lightweight 2D browser animation. These improvements strengthen scripts and quality checks; they do not turn it into a full 3D animation engine.
